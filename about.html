About Wakavuti

Compassionate Mental Health, Wellness and Rehabilitation Care

Wakavuti Blessed Medical Mental Health Wellness and Rehabilitation Limited is dedicated to supporting individuals, families and communities through professional, confidential and compassionate mental health services.
