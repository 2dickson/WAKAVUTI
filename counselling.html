Our Process

Simple, Confidential and Supportive

We follow a respectful counselling process that helps every client feel safe, understood and professionally guided.

1. Book Appointment

Contact us and choose a suitable time to begin your counselling journey.

2. Initial Session

We listen carefully, understand your concerns and identify the best support.

3. Guided Support

You receive professional guidance through structured counselling sessions.

4. Follow-up

We support your progress and help you build healthier coping strategies.
